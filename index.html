FAQ

Questions we often hear

How much does a roof replacement cost?

Every roof is different, so we survey first. As a guide for this demo, small repairs start from £150 and full replacements from £4,500. You get a fixed written price before any work starts.

How long does a new roof take?

Most homes take 3 to 7 days depending on size and weather. We confirm the timescale in your written quote.

Do you offer a guarantee?

Yes. All workmanship comes with a 10-year guarantee, and materials carry the manufacturer’s warranty.

Can you help with urgent leaks?

Yes. Choose “Urgent” in the form or call us and we aim to respond the same day.

Which areas do you cover?

We work across Essex and Greater London. Enter your post code in the quote form to check.
